1. Instrument Basics & Universal Classification

1.1 Functional Classification (applies to every specialty)

ClassKey differentiating featureKey use / representative examples
Cutting & dissectingSharp edge or point; no ratchetIncise/divide tissue — scalpel, scissors, osteotome, saw
Grasping & holdingJaws with teeth/serrations; may be locking (ratchet) or non-locking (thumb)Stabilise tissue for dissection — Allis, Babcock, dissecting forceps
Clamping & occludingRatchet + box joint; transverse serrationsStop bleeding / occlude lumen — artery forceps, bowel clamps, vascular clamps
Retracting & exposingBlade/hook/prong, no jaws; hand-held or self-retainingHold wound edges apart — Langenbeck, Deaver, Balfour, Finochietto
Dilating & probingSmooth graduated blunt shaft or expanding bladesEnlarge or explore a lumen/tract — Hegar's, urethral sounds, sinus probe
Suturing & staplingCross-hatched short jaws / cartridgeApproximate tissue — needle holders, staplers, clip appliers
SuctioningHollow tube with tip design matched to siteClear field — Yankauer, Poole, Frazier
Viewing / endoscopesOptical rod-lens or fibre system with light channelVisualise cavity — cystoscope, bronchoscope, laparoscope
Accessory / ancillaryNo tissue contact functionKidney tray, gallipot, Cheatle's forceps, drums, trolley
"C-G-C-R-D-S-S-V" — Cut, Grasp, Clamp, Retract, Dilate, Suture, Suck, View. Every instrument in the exam fits one of these 8 boxes.

1.2 Parts of a Ringed Instrument & Materials

Part / MaterialKey differentiating featureKey use / significance
Tip / jawSerrated, toothed, fenestrated or atraumaticDetermines grip strength & tissue trauma
Box lock (box joint)Interlocking box hinge — no side-playStronger, precise apposition; standard in artery forceps
Screw / pin jointSimple overlapping shanksCheaper, weaker; scissors, some forceps
Ratchet (catch)Toothed lock near finger ringsLocks jaw pressure hands-free (haemostats, needle holders)
ShankLength between joint and ringsLonger shank = deeper cavity reach
Stainless steel 18/818% chromium, 8% nickel; matte or mirror finishStandard corrosion-resistant instrument steel
Tungsten-carbide insertsGold-coloured handles/ringsMarks TC needle holders & scissors — longer life, better grip
TitaniumLight, non-magnetic, blue-grey anodisedMicrosurgery, neuro/ophthalmic, MRI-compatible implants
Chromium-plated carbon steelPlating can chip → rustCheaper/disposable-grade instruments
Gold handles = tungsten carbide · Black handles = "super-cut"/diamond-dust scissors · Green handles = supercut TC. A very common one-liner MCQ.
